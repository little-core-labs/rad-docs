# Maximize Your Earnings

The Rad TV team is working hard to get your content in front of as many consumers as possible.

For starters, all of your followers on Rad TV are notified every time you make a video public. This email provides a direct link to your new content so viewers are just 1 click away from your creations.

On top of that we are featuring creator content across our entire platform including all of our apps. Whether your content is made available for free, or within our subscriptions, we are doing our best to generate clicks and get as many eyes on your content as possible. To give your content the best chance of generating clicks here are some things to consider:

**How does your channel make you feel?**

* Are you satisfied with the presentation of your individual pieces of content and your channel to potential buyers?
* Do you have a professional channel banner?
* Do you have cover images on your videos?
* Does your content have a title that accurately describes your content to generate the click you are looking for?
  * Does your content have rich descriptions and is it clear what buyers are purchasing?

If you need to spice it up a bit, head to your [Creator Dashboard](https://rad.live/channel/) and click the Edit Channel button in the left navigation.

![](../assets/Screenshot%202024-10-28%20at%202.41.06%E2%80%AFPM.webp "Creator Dashboard")

Need to add cover photos to your videos or update titles and descriptions? Head to you [My Videos](https://rad.live/channel/) page and click on the edit button (pencil icon) to edit video metadata.

![](../assets/Screenshot%202024-10-28%20at%202.43.49%E2%80%AFPM.webp "My Videos Page")

**Do you have trailers or clips that promote and provide previews of your content?**

Trailers go a long way when promoting content. Consider creating clips or a trailer to share on socials that gets followers excited and drives clicks to your content.

**Do you have any free content available for potential buyers to check out before committing to our subscriptions?**

The freemium model works. Getting viewers in the door and showing them your awesome content is a great step towards enticing them to subscribe. Try uploading a few pieces of content and making them available for free.

**Have you promoted your content on social media?**

Whether it’s through posts on 𝕏, reels, stories or through an email newsletter - reaching out to your fan base, friends, and family directly is the best way to spread the word about your creations. Try sharing the story behind the creation. What does this piece mean to you? Why did you create it? What do you want viewers to feel?

'_Everything_ that needs to be _said has already_ been _said_. But since no one was listening, _everything_ must be _said_ again.' - Andre Gide

**PROMOTE! PROMOTE! PROMOTE!**

As creators we understand the feeling of endless promotion and how it can seem like you are just spinning your wheels. EVERY CREATOR EXPERIENCES THIS FEELING! The important thing to remember is that it won't last forever. You are creating works you love and if you don't share them with the world - how will the world learn that they exist? The thing to focus on is one connection at a time, one conversation at a time, one creation at a time - keep going! You are one comment, one share, one conversation, one creation away from your next fan.

**Collaborate with other creators or even marketers**

Consider collaborating with another creator on a piece or collection for cross-promotion and a collective tag team!
