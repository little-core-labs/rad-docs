# Create Content Playlists

1. Sign in to your Rad TV account and head to your creator studio by clicking on your profile picture in the upper right hand corner then clicking on Creator Studio.

<figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/1 Head to Creator Studio.jpeg" alt="" width="563"><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

2. After clicking on Creator Studio you will be taken to the creator studio page that looks like this 👇

<figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/2 Creator Studio Page.png" alt=""><figcaption><p>Creator Studio</p></figcaption></figure>

From here you can Upload New Videos, access Playlists, access and edit existing video metadata and monetization options, or customize your channel.

3. Click on Playlists

<figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/3 Click Playlists.jpeg" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

4. After clicking Playlists you will be taken to the My Playlists page.

<figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/4 My Playlists (1).png" alt=""><figcaption><p>My Playlists Page</p></figcaption></figure>

From here you can create and edit playlists. Let’s create a playlist!

5. Click the Create button

<figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/5 Click Create.jpeg" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

6. You will be taken to the Playlist Customization page that looks like this 👇

<figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/6 Playlist Customization .png" alt=""><figcaption><p>Playlist Customization Page</p></figcaption></figure>

7. The data inserted into this form will be used to create your playlist.

<figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/7 Playlist Metadata.png" alt=""><figcaption><p>Playlist Metadata</p></figcaption></figure>

Add a Title, description including any links, and a thumbnail that is 1920x1080.

8. Now it’s time to add content to your playlist. ([Click here](upload-and-monetize-videos.md) to learn how to upload content.) Click the dropdown arrow to select a piece of content to add to your playlist.

<figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/8 Click the dropdown arrow.JPG" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

9. Select a piece of content.

<figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/9 Select a piece of content (1).png" alt=""><figcaption><p>Select Content to Add</p></figcaption></figure>

10. Click Add Item to add the selected content to your playlist.

<figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/10 Click Add Item.JPG" alt=""><figcaption><p>Adding Content to Playlists</p></figcaption></figure>

11. After clicking Add Item you will see that the piece of content selected and added has been added to your playlist.

<figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/11 After clicking Add Item.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

12. Let's add a second piece of content by clicking the dropdown menu and selecting another piece of content.

<figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/12 Select a second piece of content.jpeg" alt=""><figcaption><p>Adding Additional Content to Playlists</p></figcaption></figure>

Select an additional piece of content from the dropdown menu 👇

<figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/13 Select the content you want to add.png" alt=""><figcaption><p>Select Another Piece of Content to Add</p></figcaption></figure>

Click Add Item again 👇

<figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/14 Click Add Item.JPG" alt=""><figcaption><p>Click Add Item</p></figcaption></figure>

After clicking Add Item you will see that a second piece of content has been added to the playlist.

<figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/15 Second item to Playlist.png" alt=""><figcaption><p>Playlist Customization Page Showing a Playlist with 2 Pieces of Content</p></figcaption></figure>

Drag and drop content to change the order of content in your playlist or click the trash can icon to delete a piece of content from a playlist.

<figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/16 Drag and Drop + Delete Content.JPG" alt=""><figcaption><p>Playlist Customization Options</p></figcaption></figure>

Repeat steps 8 through 10 to add as many pieces of content to a playlist as you would like.

13. Once you have added the content to your playlist click the Create Playlist button.&#x20;

<figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/17 Click Create Playlist Button.JPG" alt=""><figcaption><p>Finalize Playlist Creation</p></figcaption></figure>

14. After you click Create Playlist you will see a green ✅ indicating that your playlist has been created successfully.

<figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/18 Playlist Created Successfully.png" alt=""><figcaption><p>Playlist Created Successfully</p></figcaption></figure>

15. After you have successfully created a playlist you can view your new Home tab on your public facing creator page. To navigate to your channel click the profile pic image. Once you hover over the profile image you will see text pop up that says “View Channel”.

<figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/19 Click pfp to view channel and new home tab.jpeg" alt=""><figcaption><p>View Playlists on Your Home Page</p></figcaption></figure>

16. Once you click the pfp to view your channel you will be taken to the new Home tab on your creator profile. The ‘Home’ tab is only available if you have created a playlist. You can see the playlist we just created and the videos it contains.&#x20;

<figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/20 Home Tab for custom Playlists (1).png" alt=""><figcaption><p>Home Tab View for Playlists</p></figcaption></figure>

17. To edit existing playlists head back to your creator studio and navigate to the Playlists section.

<figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/3 Click Playlists (1).jpeg" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

You will be taken to your playlists page. To edit a playlist click the pencil icon.

<figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/21 Click the pencil to edit your playlist.jpeg" alt=""><figcaption><p>Edit Playlists</p></figcaption></figure>

That’s it! Great work! You are now a Rad TV playlist pro! [Click here](sell-individual-videos.md) to learn about monetizing your content on Rad TV.
